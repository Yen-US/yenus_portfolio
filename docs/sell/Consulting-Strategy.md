# Consulting — Simple Consulting Strategy

## Strategy in one sentence

Keep the current job, protect financial runway, and sell one small, fixed-price diagnostic that turns a real business bottleneck into a clear implementation plan—and naturally leads to larger build work.

---

# 1. The Offer

## Consulting Fix Plan

> **Bring us one digital process that is wasting time, losing customers, or creating chaos. In one focused session, we’ll identify the bottleneck and give you a practical plan to fix it.**

### Examples

- “Customers keep asking the same questions on WhatsApp.”
- “We lose reservations between Instagram, WhatsApp, and email.”
- “Our website gets visits but few inquiries.”
- “Creating and publishing content takes too much time.”
- “Client approvals and follow-ups are disorganized.”
- “Staff copy information between systems manually.”

Do not lead with AI. Lead with the annoying, expensive problem they already recognize.

---

# 2. What the Client Gets

## Fixed scope

1. **45-minute working session**
   - Understand the current process.
   - Identify where time, leads, or information are being lost.
   - Establish what a useful improvement would look like.

2. **One-page Fix Plan within 48 hours**
   - Current bottleneck.
   - Recommended solution.
   - Simple workflow diagram.
   - Tools or integrations required.
   - Estimated implementation range.
   - Risks, assumptions, and next step.

3. **15-minute follow-up**
   - Answer questions.
   - Decide whether they should implement it themselves, hire Consulting, or leave it alone.

## Price

> **Launch price: $150 fixed.**

If the client hires Consulting to implement the recommendation within 30 days, the full $150 is credited toward the project.

No retainer. No long contract. No vague consulting engagement.

---

# 3. Why This Is Easy to Buy

- The client brings **one problem**, not their entire company.
- The price and timeline are fixed.
- The commitment is under one hour initially.
- They receive a useful artifact even if they do not hire Consulting afterward.
- They do not need to understand AI, software architecture, or automation.
- The implementation credit reduces the feeling of paying twice.
- Consulting can reject problems that do not justify a build.

The sale is not:

> “Would you like an AI transformation strategy?”

It is:

> “What repetitive or disorganized part of your business annoys you every week? I’ll map the simplest way to fix one of them.”

---

# 4. Ideal Client

Start with businesses where access and trust already exist:

- boutique hotels and property managers;
- restaurants and hospitality operators;
- tour companies and surf schools;
- galleries and creative businesses;
- real-estate teams;
- professional-service firms;
- founders in Yenson’s Microsoft/startup network.

## Qualification

A strong Fix Plan candidate has:

- a repeated problem, not a one-time inconvenience;
- a person who owns the problem;
- visible cost in time, missed leads, errors, or frustration;
- enough urgency to discuss it this month;
- a plausible solution within Consulting’s capabilities.

Do not sell a Fix Plan when the prospect only wants free brainstorming, has no authority, or cannot describe a recurring problem.

# 5. The Low-Commitment Hook

## Primary message

> **What is one part of your business that still depends on messages, spreadsheets, or someone remembering to follow up? I’m offering a small fixed-price session where I map the bottleneck and give you a one-page plan to fix it. No long contract, and if we build it, the session is credited toward the project.**

## Short WhatsApp version

> I’m testing a new Consulting service for local businesses. You bring me one messy digital process—bookings, WhatsApp follow-up, content, client approvals, anything repetitive—and I give you a practical one-page fix plan in 48 hours. It’s $150, and the full amount is credited if we implement it. Know anyone with a process that drives them mad?

## Spanish version

> Estoy probando un servicio nuevo de Consulting para negocios. Me traés un proceso digital desordenado—reservas, seguimiento por WhatsApp, contenido, aprobaciones de clientes o cualquier tarea repetitiva—y en 48 horas te entrego un plan práctico de una página para resolverlo. Cuesta $150 y se acredita completo si después lo implementamos. ¿Conocés a alguien con un proceso que le complique la vida todas las semanas?

## In-person opener

> What part of the business still feels unnecessarily manual or chaotic?

Listen first. Mention the offer only after a real recurring pain appears.

# 6. The Natural Expansion Path

The Fix Plan is the wedge, not the final business model.

```text
Recurring pain
    ↓
$150 Consulting Fix Plan
    ↓
Clear recommendation and implementation range
    ↓
Fixed-scope build
    ↓
Optional support or improvement retainer
```

## Implementation bands

Do not quote before the Fix Plan is complete. Use these internal bands:

- **Small fix:** $500–$1,500
  - landing page improvement;
  - lead form and notifications;
  - basic WhatsApp/email workflow;
  - simple booking or approval flow.

- **Workflow build:** $1,500–$5,000
  - multi-step automation;
  - internal dashboard;
  - customer portal;
  - CRM or booking integration;
  - content operations workflow.

- **Custom system:** $5,000+
  - custom software product;
  - AI agent or RAG system;
  - multiple integrations;
  - security, evaluation, and production architecture.

The client buys the next step only when the evidence justifies it.

> **Consulting fixes the digital processes that slow growing businesses down.**

## Supporting line

> From websites and customer follow-up to internal tools and AI systems, we find the simplest useful solution—and build it properly when needed.

## Yenson’s credibility

Use restrained facts:

- Senior AI and cloud architecture experience.
- Work supporting Microsoft for Startups through Accenture.
- Experience translating founder problems into production-ready systems.
- Local presence in Tamarindo with bilingual delivery.
- Real software, architecture, and automation capability beyond template websites.

Do not lead with Fortune 500, autonomous agents, or an exhaustive technology stack. Credibility supports the offer; it is not the offer.

# 9. Thirty-Day Sales Experiment

## Goal

Prove whether this offer creates qualified conversations and implementation opportunities without consuming the freedom Consulting is meant to protect.

## Weekly commitments

- 10 personal or warm outreach messages.
- 3 in-person conversations with business owners or operators.
- 2 LinkedIn posts showing a real bottleneck and practical fix.
- 1 direct referral request.
- Follow up with every interested lead within one business day.

## Track

- outreach sent;
- replies;
- qualified conversations;
- Fix Plans sold;
- Fix Plans delivered;
- implementation proposals;
- projects won;
- booked revenue;
- collected revenue;
- delivery hours;
- founder energy and stress.

## Thirty-day review

Continue or improve the offer if:

- people immediately understand it;
- at least three paid Fix Plans sell;
- at least one produces a qualified implementation opportunity;
- delivery remains repeatable and profitable;
- the work reveals recurring problems worth productizing.

Change the market, message, price, or offer if conversations occur but buyers do not convert.

Escalate the selective job search if the offer produces no meaningful signal, runway falls below the safety floor, or monthly burn exceeds the agreed ceiling.

---

# 10. Boundaries

- Keep the current job.
- Do not add another job merely to reduce uncertainty.
- Do not build Company OS before it supports an already-proven workflow.
- Do not create custom demos for unqualified prospects.
- Do not perform unpaid audits disguised as sales calls.
- Do not accept broad “fix my whole business” engagements.
- Do not finance client work or business experiments with additional card debt.
- Do not let Consulting become five different companies at once.

One offer. One problem. One useful artifact. One natural next step.
